Permissions Policy

1. User Roles & Permissions

We classify users into several roles, each with specific permissions:

  • Guest Users:
    • May only read content and explore the site.
    • May contact us via the "Contact Me" page or email.
  • Registered Users:
    • Can do everything a guest can (read, explore).
    • May comment on posts and engage in chat with other users.
    • Can view and delete their profile.
  • Admin Users:
    • Have access to all permissions granted to Registered Users.
    • May create posts and view admin-only pages such as complaints and user reports.
  • Owner:
    • Holds supreme control over the site.
    • May update code, grant admin access, ban users, update or delete posts, and address user complaints.
    • Can perform all actions an Admin can, and has ultimate control over the platform's functionality.

2. Content Uploads and Restrictions

Only Admins and Owners have the ability to upload content (e.g., creating or editing posts). Users are prohibited from uploading any content to the site unless explicitly authorized.

3. Chat System Rules

We aim to maintain a respectful and positive environment. Therefore, all users must comply with the following guidelines while engaging in chat:

  • No swearing, cursing, or offensive language.
  • Respect others' rights and engage in respectful conversations.
  • Do not post external links, unsolicited promotions, or spam.
  • No personal information should be shared in the chat, including addresses, phone numbers, and emails.
  • Harassment or torturing of other users is strictly prohibited.
  • Users must adhere to privacy: sharing personal data of other users without consent is not allowed.

Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)

1. Purpose

This Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) outlines the acceptable and prohibited activities for all users of the platform. By using our platform, users agree to follow these guidelines to ensure a safe and respectful online community.

2. Prohibited Activities and Content

The following activities and content are strictly prohibited:

  • Illegal content: Any content related to illegal activities, including but not limited to hacking, fraud, and violation of intellectual property rights.
  • Abusive, harassing, or harmful content: Content that threatens, harasses, or violates the rights of others, including bullying, abuse, or threats, is not permitted.
  • Spam: The posting of unsolicited messages, including advertisements, is prohibited.
  • Impersonation: Users must not impersonate other individuals, including platform staff or other users.
  • Violation of privacy: Users may not sell or share personal data without explicit permission. Scraping, bots, or automated systems to extract or collect information from the platform is not allowed.
  • Harassment: Any form of harassment, abuse, or threats, including torture or degrading behavior, is strictly prohibited.

3. User Conduct

By using this platform, users agree to behave respectfully and in compliance with the following:

  • Respect others’ privacy: Do not share sensitive personal data of others.
  • Keep it respectful: Users are expected to treat others with kindness and respect, regardless of their background or opinions.
  • Avoid personal attacks: Constructive criticism is encouraged, but personal attacks, name-calling, and inflammatory language are not acceptable.

4. Account Security

Password Protection: All passwords are hashed for security. Users are responsible for ensuring the confidentiality of their accounts.

Unauthorized access: Users may not attempt to access accounts or content that is not their own.

Reporting breaches: Any unauthorized or suspicious activity must be reported to the site administrators immediately.

5. Enforcement and Violations

Users who violate any part of this AUP may face the following actions:

  • Warnings: Minor violations may result in warnings.
  • Temporary bans: Severe or repeated violations may lead to a temporary ban.
  • Permanent bans: For serious offenses, such as illegal activities, permanent bans will be imposed without prior warning.
  • Appeal Process: There is no formal appeal process for violations of this policy, as enforcement is strict.

6. Ownership and Control

Admin rights: Admin users may moderate content, address user complaints, and manage posts and comments.

Owner rights: The platform’s owner holds supreme control over all functions, including banning users, granting admin privileges, and managing the site's code and functionality.

7. Updates

The Terms & Conditions and Acceptable Use Policy will be updated occasionally to reflect changes to the platform's functionality or legal requirements. The date issued will be updated to indicate when the document was last revised.

Users will be notified of major updates to these documents.
